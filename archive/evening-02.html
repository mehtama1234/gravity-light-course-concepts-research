The evening lecture frames why gravity and quantum theory strain against each other.

First-Principles Role

This evening lecture sits at the pressure point between the course's classical geometry and quantum matter. The ordinary problem is not that two famous theories have different branding. It is that general relativity treats spacetime geometry as a classical field while quantum theory changes what can be meant by a definite matter source.

The operation is comparison: ask what each theory holds fixed, what it lets vary, and what kind of measurement claim it allows. That comparison exposes why quantizing gravity is not a small patch to Einstein's equation.
