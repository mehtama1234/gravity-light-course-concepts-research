Tutorial 3: Multilinear Algebra

The tutorial makes students handle slot-aware quantities instead of treating every symbol as a vector or matrix.

Mathematical Object

Vectors, covectors, tensors, bases, components, and the multilinear slots that give each object its job.

Operation

Feed objects into slots, change bases, and check which parts are the object and which parts are only its display.

Why It Matters

The metric, curvature, and stress-energy tensor all carry their meaning through slots and transformations.

What Breaks Without It

Without this practice, Einstein's equation becomes symbol matching rather than a claim about geometric and physical measuring rules.
