Lecture 16: Lecture 16: Optical Geometry I

How does this lecture use Optical Geometry and Null Geodesics, Geodesics As Free Motion, Metric Signature And Light Cones, Redshift As Stretched Light, Gravitational Lensing And Focusing to move the course from everyday geometric intuition toward a usable theory of gravity and light?

Family Role

Read spacetime with light: The course needs a bridge from equations to observation. Light is the bridge because it follows the causal structure set by the metric and carries information to detectors.

First-Principles Role

Lecture 16 should be read as a step in the course's construction, not as an isolated topic. The ordinary problem is: The course needs to explain why light follows paths fixed by spacetime geometry rather than by an external optical medium. The course needs a precise replacement for straight-line motion when spacetime may be curved. The course needs a measurement rule that separates possible time-like motion, space-like separation, and light-like travel. The course needs to explain how geometry changes the light that finally reaches an observer. The course needs to explain why masses can change apparent positions and brightness of distant sources. The mathematical work to watch is: The operation is solving the geodesic equation under the null condition and comparing neighboring light rays through focusing or lensing. The operation is checking whether the curve's tangent has zero covariant change along the curve. The operation is evaluating the metric on tangent directions and sorting them into time-like, space-like, or null. The operation is comparing the light's wave direction with the emitter's and observer's time directions. The operation is tracking neighboring light paths and measuring how the bundle changes direction, area, or shape. A good writeup should make that operation visible before using later terms from relativity.

Objects To Track

  • Null geodesics are curves whose tangent has zero metric length and is parallel transported along itself.
  • A geodesic is a curve whose tangent direction is transported along itself according to the connection.
  • Lorentzian signature is the metric's time-space sign pattern, and light cones are the resulting boundaries of causal influence.
  • Redshift is the change in measured light frequency between emission and observation along a null path.
  • Lensing is the bending and focusing of families of null geodesics by spacetime curvature.
