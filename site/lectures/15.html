Lecture 15: Lecture 15: Einstein Gravity

How does this lecture use Matter and Stress-Energy, Einstein Equation to move the course from everyday geometric intuition toward a usable theory of gravity and light?

Family Role

Make laws coordinate-free: Once geometry exists, the course needs laws that can be stated without picking a favorite observer or map. It also needs a precise object for what matter contributes to gravity.

First-Principles Role

Lecture 15 should be read as a step in the course's construction, not as an isolated topic. The ordinary problem is: Gravity needs a precise account of what matter contributes: not just mass, but energy, momentum, pressure, and stresses. The course needs the law that says how spacetime geometry and matter constrain each other. The mathematical work to watch is: The operation is evaluating its slots against observer directions and surface normals, then requiring local conservation through covariant divergence. The operation is equating the geometric tensor to stress-energy, then solving the resulting coupled nonlinear field equations under boundary or symmetry assumptions. A good writeup should make that operation visible before using later terms from relativity.

Objects To Track

  • The stress-energy tensor is the object that measures energy density, momentum flow, pressure, and stress through chosen directions or surfaces.
  • The Einstein tensor is built from curvature and the metric so that its covariant divergence vanishes, matching local conservation of stress-energy.
