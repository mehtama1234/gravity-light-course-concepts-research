Lecture 05: Lecture 5: Tangent Spaces

How does this lecture use Tangent Spaces and Fields to move the course from everyday geometric intuition toward a usable theory of gravity and light?

Family Role

Build space before measuring it: A theory of gravity cannot begin by assuming a fixed grid, fixed rulers, or a surrounding room. These lectures build the minimum language needed to speak about places, change, and physical quantities before distance has been granted.

First-Principles Role

Lecture 05 should be read as a step in the course's construction, not as an isolated topic. The ordinary problem is: The course needs a way to attach possible directions of motion to every event, then let physical quantities vary from event to event. The mathematical work to watch is: The basic operation is differentiating a function along a tangent direction and then doing this smoothly point by point across the manifold. A good writeup should make that operation visible before using later terms from relativity.

Objects To Track

  • A tangent space is the vector space of directions available at one point; a field assigns a value, such as a vector or tensor, to each point.
