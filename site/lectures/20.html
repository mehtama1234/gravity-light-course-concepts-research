Lecture 20: Lecture 20: Cosmology - The early epoch

How does this lecture use Cosmology and Expansion to move the course from everyday geometric intuition toward a usable theory of gravity and light?

Family Role

Solve evolution and global shape: The course then asks how whole spacetimes behave: how valid initial data evolves, how the universe expands, and how causal boundaries such as horizons are understood.

First-Principles Role

Lecture 20 should be read as a step in the course's construction, not as an isolated topic. The ordinary problem is: The course needs to apply the field equation to a whole-universe model without pretending every detail of the universe is tracked. The mathematical work to watch is: The operation is imposing homogeneity/isotropy, deriving reduced evolution equations, and reading matter content from how the scale factor changes. A good writeup should make that operation visible before using later terms from relativity.

Objects To Track

  • A cosmological model uses symmetry assumptions plus a scale factor to reduce Einstein's equation to equations for large-scale evolution.
