Lecture 06: Lecture 6: Fields

How does this lecture use Tangent Spaces and Fields, Vector Fields As Local Instructions to move the course from everyday geometric intuition toward a usable theory of gravity and light?

Family Role

Build space before measuring it: A theory of gravity cannot begin by assuming a fixed grid, fixed rulers, or a surrounding room. These lectures build the minimum language needed to speak about places, change, and physical quantities before distance has been granted.

First-Principles Role

Lecture 06 should be read as a step in the course's construction, not as an isolated topic. The ordinary problem is: The course needs a way to attach possible directions of motion to every event, then let physical quantities vary from event to event. The course needs a way to assign a possible motion or instruction at every event in a region. The mathematical work to watch is: The basic operation is differentiating a function along a tangent direction and then doing this smoothly point by point across the manifold. The operation is following the assigned directions to form flow lines or using the field to differentiate other quantities. A good writeup should make that operation visible before using later terms from relativity.

Objects To Track

  • A tangent space is the vector space of directions available at one point; a field assigns a value, such as a vector or tensor, to each point.
  • A vector field assigns one tangent vector to each point of the manifold in a smooth way.
