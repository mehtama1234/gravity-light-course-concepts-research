Lecture 10: Lecture 10: Metric Manifolds

How does this lecture use Metric Manifolds to move the course from everyday geometric intuition toward a usable theory of gravity and light?

Family Role

Turn falling into geometry: The course must explain free motion without saying that every path is judged against an invisible flat background. It needs its own rule for straightness and its own test for real gravitational curvature.

First-Principles Role

Lecture 10 should be read as a step in the course's construction, not as an isolated topic. The ordinary problem is: After building nearness and smoothness, the course needs a rule that tells clocks, rulers, and light cones what they measure. The mathematical work to watch is: The operation is evaluating the metric on tangent vectors to compute intervals, norms, angles, volumes, and null directions. A good writeup should make that operation visible before using later terms from relativity.

Objects To Track

  • A metric is a smoothly varying inner product on tangent spaces, with Lorentzian signature in spacetime so that time and space enter differently.
