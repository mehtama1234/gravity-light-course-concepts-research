Lecture 13: Lecture 13: Spacetime

How does this lecture use Metric Manifolds to move the course from everyday geometric intuition toward a usable theory of gravity and light?

Family Role

Make laws coordinate-free: Once geometry exists, the course needs laws that can be stated without picking a favorite observer or map. It also needs a precise object for what matter contributes to gravity.

First-Principles Role

Lecture 13 should be read as a step in the course's construction, not as an isolated topic. The ordinary problem is: After building nearness and smoothness, the course needs a rule that tells clocks, rulers, and light cones what they measure. The mathematical work to watch is: The operation is evaluating the metric on tangent vectors to compute intervals, norms, angles, volumes, and null directions. A good writeup should make that operation visible before using later terms from relativity.

Objects To Track

  • A metric is a smoothly varying inner product on tangent spaces, with Lorentzian signature in spacetime so that time and space enter differently.
