Lecture 07: Lecture 7: Connections

How does this lecture use Connections and Parallel Transport, Covariant Derivative Corrects Comparison, Geodesics As Free Motion to move the course from everyday geometric intuition toward a usable theory of gravity and light?

Family Role

Turn falling into geometry: The course must explain free motion without saying that every path is judged against an invisible flat background. It needs its own rule for straightness and its own test for real gravitational curvature.

First-Principles Role

Lecture 07 should be read as a step in the course's construction, not as an isolated topic. The ordinary problem is: To compare directions at different points, the course needs a rule for carrying a direction along a path. The course needs a derivative of vector fields that does not compare vectors as if all tangent spaces were the same room. The course needs a precise replacement for straight-line motion when spacetime may be curved. The mathematical work to watch is: The central operation is covariant differentiation: measuring how a field changes after subtracting the change caused merely by moving through tangent spaces. The operation is differentiating the field and subtracting the change caused by the moving basis or comparison rule. The operation is checking whether the curve's tangent has zero covariant change along the curve. A good writeup should make that operation visible before using later terms from relativity.

Objects To Track

  • A connection is a rule for differentiating vector fields along directions and for transporting vectors from one tangent space to another.
  • A covariant derivative is the corrected change of a vector field along a direction, using a connection to say how nearby tangent spaces are compared.
  • A geodesic is a curve whose tangent direction is transported along itself according to the connection.
