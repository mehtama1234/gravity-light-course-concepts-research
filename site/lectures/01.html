Lecture 01: Lecture 1: Topology

How does this lecture use Topology and Continuity to move the course from everyday geometric intuition toward a usable theory of gravity and light?

Family Role

Build space before measuring it: A theory of gravity cannot begin by assuming a fixed grid, fixed rulers, or a surrounding room. These lectures build the minimum language needed to speak about places, change, and physical quantities before distance has been granted.

First-Principles Role

Lecture 01 should be read as a step in the course's construction, not as an isolated topic. The ordinary problem is: Before measuring distance, the course needs a way to say which points are near enough for a limiting process to make sense. The mathematical work to watch is: The basic operation is checking whether inverse images of open sets are open, which is the coordinate-free test that a map preserves nearness. A good writeup should make that operation visible before using later terms from relativity.
