Lecture 12: Lecture 12: Integration on manifolds

How does this lecture use Integration on Manifolds to move the course from everyday geometric intuition toward a usable theory of gravity and light?

Family Role

Make laws coordinate-free: Once geometry exists, the course needs laws that can be stated without picking a favorite observer or map. It also needs a precise object for what matter contributes to gravity.

First-Principles Role

Lecture 12 should be read as a step in the course's construction, not as an isolated topic. The ordinary problem is: The course needs a way to add local quantities over curved spaces without depending on one coordinate chart. The mathematical work to watch is: The operation is pulling forms back to charts or submanifolds, integrating them there, and using Stokes-type relations between boundary and bulk. A good writeup should make that operation visible before using later terms from relativity.

Objects To Track

  • Differential forms and volume elements package what can be integrated in a coordinate-independent way.
